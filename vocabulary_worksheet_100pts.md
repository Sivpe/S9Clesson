### Part 2 (20 pts / 4 pts each)
1. **Shelter** (2 pts). *Explanation (2 pts):* A shelter is a basic structure designed specifically to provide cover/protection from adverse weather, whereas a shed is for tool storage and a hut is a basic habitable dwelling.
2. **Complex** (2 pts). *Explanation (2 pts):* A complex refers to a large grouping or interconnected network of multiple buildings/facilities, not just a single venue.
3. **Hand rails** (2 pts). *Explanation (2 pts):* Hand rails are narrow rails designed to be gripped by hands for balance while walking along steps/stairs, whereas barriers are safety barricades to block access or prevent falls.
4. **Ladder** (2 pts). *Explanation (2 pts):* A ladder consists of vertical rungs requiring two-hand climbing, whereas steps are fixed horizontal flat surfaces you walk up.
5. **Community centre** (2 pts). *Explanation (2 pts):* A community centre is an inclusive public space serving all demographic groups across the neighbourhood, whereas a youth club caters specifically to teenagers.

- **Task B (10 pts / 2 pts each):**

  2. *roof* -> **basement** (Underground area is the basement, not the roof).

  5. *pulled up* -> **did up** (*Pull down* = demolish; *do up* = redecorate/renovate).
