# Prepare B2 (Level Secondary S9C) — Unit 20 Teaching Suite
## "Places to Hang Out" & "A Place of Your Own" (Student's Book Pages 112–113)

Welcome to the complete teaching bundle for **Prepare B2, Unit 20 (Secondary Level S9C)**. This repository contains an interactive digital presentation platform, classroom management tools, teacher scripting, and two standalone 100-point assessment worksheets with full answer keys and pedagogical rationales.

---

## 📂 Repository Contents

1. **`index.html`** — Interactive Web Application & Slide Deck:
   - **Slide Presentation Mode:** 12 full-screen classroom slides with step-by-step answer reveals, group work timers, phonetics/pronunciation guides, and live teacher scripts.
   - **Vocabulary Worksheet (100 Points):** Digital interactive quiz with instant scoring, feedback, and printable styling.
   - **Reading Comprehension Worksheet (100 Points):** Full text side-by-side reader, Inverted Question Formulations, B2 inference questions, and analytical essay rubric.
   - **Teacher's Master Guide & Keys:** Full lesson timeline, Concept Checking Questions (CCQs), and 100% complete answer keys.
2. **`lesson_plan_and_slides.md`** — Comprehensive Teacher Lesson Plan with step-by-step classroom timing (60–90 min), board work, CCQs, and differentiation strategies.
3. **`vocabulary_worksheet_100pts.md`** — Printable 100-point vocabulary test paper covering all 15 target words, nuance distinctions, phrasal verbs, and B2 sentence transformations.
4. **`reading_worksheet_100pts.md`** — Printable 100-point reading comprehension test paper featuring Gist, Inverted Question Matching, Inference, Register, and Comparative Essay.

## 🎯 Pedagogical Overview & Learning Outcomes

- **Target Level:** CEFR B2 (Secondary S9C)
- **Target Vocabulary (15 items):**
  - *Buildings & Enclosures:* `arts complex`, `community centre`, `hut`, `shed`, `shelter`, `basement`
  - *Architectural & Structural Elements:* `frame`, `chimney`, `steps`, `ladder`, `bench`, `rug`, `surface`, `concrete`, `barriers`
  - *Phrasal Verbs & Idioms:* `do up`, `pull down`, `chill out`, `hunt for`, `take someone to court`
- **Reading Core Skill:**
  - Inverted Question Deduction: Reconstructing interview questions from speaker statements based on contextual and grammatical clues.
  - Comparative Text Synthesis: Evaluating youth autonomy, legal liability, and sustainability.

## 🚀 How to Run & View

- **Live Classroom Preview:** The live interactive app is running at port `3000`. You can project it onto an interactive whiteboard or student tablets.
